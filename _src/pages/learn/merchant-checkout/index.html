Take your first sale

Price a sale, present the invoice and confirm that the customer paid before closing the order.

Set up the counter

Open Terminal with the wallet you intend to use for the business. In Terminal Settings → Currency, choose EUR, USD, CHF, BTC or sats. Fiat pricing sets the price presented at checkout. You receive bitcoin. For a €4.50 coffee, you can enter the price directly on the keypad.

For repeat orders, add products under Settings → Items and combine them in a basket. The free catalog holds five items. Cashier mode restricts access on the device you hand to staff. The owner keeps control of refunds and settings.

Accept a payment

  1. Enter the total or build the basket. Check the currency and amount with the customer.
  2. Tap Generate invoice. The customer scans the QR using a compatible Lightning wallet and approves payment.
  3. Wait for confirmation on your own Terminal. Open the paid sale to share a receipt. At the end of the shift, find the transaction under Sales.

Handle an interrupted checkout

Leaving the invoice screen can leave the invoice payable. Use Resume invoice when available. If Terminal cannot verify the result or the invoice has expired, use Check payment when shown. A verification error means the result is still uncertain. Check the original sale before asking the customer to pay again.

If euro invoice creation fails while the rate service is unavailable, try again when a fresh quote is available. A customer and merchant who agree on a price in sats can use sats pricing directly.

Common questions

Can multiple employees use separate phones?

The cashier lock applies to one local device. Sales and catalog records are stored for the active wallet and network on that device. Shared staff accounts and synchronized registers are outside this workflow.

What does the PDF receipt cover?

It records the payment in Terminal. Keep using the fiscal receipt and bookkeeping process required for your business, and confirm with your accountant how to record a bitcoin sale.
