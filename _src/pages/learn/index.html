For wallet users

  • How do I receive my first bitcoin?

    Open Receive in the on-chain wallet, choose a fresh address and share its QR code. For a Lightning payment, switch to the Lightning wallet first.

    Read the steps
  • Why are there two balances?

    On-chain funds and Lightning spending funds appear separately. Use the top-up flow to fund Lightning, or withdraw to a Bitcoin address to move funds back on-chain. Review the quoted terms first.

    Read the steps
  • How do I pay a Lightning invoice?

    Scan the merchant’s invoice from the Lightning wallet, review the amount and fee, then confirm once. If the outcome is unclear, check both wallets before trying again.

    Read the steps
  • What happens to my change?

    When you spend part of an on-chain output, the remainder after the payment and fee returns to a change address in your wallet. Coin control lets you choose the outputs an on-chain payment spends.

    Read the steps
  • Can I sign with an offline wallet?

    A watch-only wallet can prepare a PSBT. Review and sign it on a compatible external signer, then import the signed transaction into Trustless to broadcast it.

    Read the steps

For merchants

  • Does my customer need Trustless to pay?

    They can scan your invoice with a compatible Lightning wallet. Enter the price, generate the invoice and wait for confirmation on your own Terminal.

    Read the steps
  • The customer paid. Why is the sale still pending?

    Resume the original invoice and use Check payment when it appears. A verification error leaves the result uncertain. Check that sale before asking the customer to pay again.

    Read the steps
  • How do I give a full or partial refund?

    Open the paid sale as the owner and ask for a Lightning invoice from the customer. Enter the refund in sats, review the fee and total, then authorize the payment.

    Read the steps
  • Can I export sales for my accountant?

    Yes. Choose the period in Sales and export the raw CSV for free. Sales retain their original price and reporting-rate information. Completed refunds are reported separately.

    Read the steps
  • Do I need a subscription to accept bitcoin?

    Basic checkout, refunds, receipts, sales CSVs and encrypted backups are free. Pro adds an unlimited catalog, detailed reports, receipt logos and reusable templates.

    Read the steps

Recovery

  • How do I recover after losing my phone?

    Recover the original wallet with its phrase. Merchant records need a separate encrypted Terminal backup file and its password. Restore it into the same wallet and network before taking new sales.

    Read the steps
