Does the customer need Trustless?

They can pay the invoice using a compatible Lightning wallet. Confirm that the sale shows as paid on the merchant device before completing the order. See the cashier walkthrough.

What is included for free?

Checkout, five catalog items, cashier lock, sales history, refunds, basic receipts, CSV export and encrypted backup are included. Terminal Pro adds unlimited catalog items, detailed reports, report presets, receipt logos and reusable templates. Compare free and Pro.

What if a payment still shows as pending?

Open the existing sale and check its status again after connectivity returns. Give the original payment time to reconcile before asking the customer to retry. Review checkout and payment states.

Can I use the PDF as my fiscal receipt?

Terminal creates a payment receipt and sales records. Your accountant and register provider can confirm how a Bitcoin sale and any refund fit your local fiscal receipt process. Understand the sales records.
