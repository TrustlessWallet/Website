Before your first payment

Trustless creates a wallet on first launch. A bitcoin wallet holds the keys that authorize spending. Your recovery phrase is a human-readable backup of those keys. Someone who obtains the phrase can spend from the wallet.

  1. Open the wallet options and choose Backup wallet.
  2. Write the words down in order and keep them somewhere private. A photograph or a message can create copies on other devices.
  3. Complete the phrase check offered by the app. Keep the backup available to you even if the phone is lost.

Receive bitcoin

An on-chain transfer goes to a Bitcoin address. A Lightning payment uses a Lightning request. Agree on the payment method with the sender before sharing a code.

  1. For an on-chain transfer, select the on-chain wallet and open Receive. Choose an unused address.
  2. Show the QR or share the address with the sender. If you copy and paste, compare the address at both ends.
  3. Open your history to check the incoming transaction. An unconfirmed transaction is waiting for inclusion in a block. For an everyday Lightning payment, follow the Lightning guide below.

Make your first payment

Start with an amount you can comfortably use for a practice purchase. On-chain, scan or enter the recipient’s address, enter the amount and review the network fee. Check the destination and the total before confirming. Bitcoin transactions generally cannot be recalled after broadcast.

Common questions

How much is a sat?

One bitcoin equals 100,000,000 satoshis. For example, 10,000 sats equals 0.0001 BTC.

Can I monitor a wallet whose keys are elsewhere?

Import its supported public key as a watch-only wallet. You can track activity and prepare an on-chain transaction for a compatible external signer. Spending requires the signing keys.
