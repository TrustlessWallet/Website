For wallet users

  • How do I receive my first bitcoin?

    Open Receive in the on-chain wallet, choose a fresh address and share its QR code. For a Lightning payment, switch to the Lightning wallet first.

  • Why are there two balances?

    On-chain funds and Lightning spending funds appear separately. Use the top-up flow to fund Lightning, or withdraw to a Bitcoin address to move funds back on-chain. Review the quoted terms first.

    Read the steps
    transfer move top up topup fund withdraw withdrawal swap lightning balance fees
  • How do I pay a Lightning invoice?

    Scan the merchant’s invoice from the Lightning wallet, review the amount and fee, then confirm once. If the outcome is unclear, check both wallets before trying again.

    Read the steps
    send scan spend pay friend purchase BOLT11 QR coffee
  • What happens to my change?

    When you spend part of an on-chain output, the remainder after the payment and fee returns to a change address in your wallet. Coin control lets you choose the outputs an on-chain payment spends.

    Read the steps
    UTXO inputs output coin control privacy reuse addresses change coins
  • Can I sign with an offline wallet?

    A watch-only wallet can prepare a PSBT. Review and sign it on a compatible external signer, then import the signed transaction into Trustless to broadcast it.
