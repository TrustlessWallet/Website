Refund and reconcile a sale

Return all or part of a payment, close the till and interpret exchange rates in your sales CSV.

On this page

Issue a full or partial refund

  1. As the owner, open the paid sale in Sales and its refund screen. Check the available amount in sats.
  2. Ask the customer to generate a receiving BOLT11 Lightning invoice for the refund. Scan or paste it and enter the refund amount in whole sats.
  3. Choose Review refund. Check the destination, amount the customer receives, network fee and total debited. Then choose Authorize refund.

A refund is a new outgoing payment. If its outcome is pending or unknown, use Check on the existing attempt. Further refunds on that sale stay blocked until the uncertainty is resolved.

Close a shift

In Sales, select Today or a custom date range. Review paid sales and completed refunds separately, then export the raw Sales CSV. Keep the exported records with the rest of your business records. Basic filtering and export are available in the free Terminal.

Sales are grouped by the time your device first observes completion, and refunds by their recorded status update. A refund issued the next day can appear in a different reporting period from the sale.

Read the recorded rates

A fiat-priced sale preserves its original price and rate. A refund has its own reporting-rate snapshot. The CSV includes the rate currency, source, observation time and status. A stale or missing rate is marked accordingly. Exporting an old record does not look up a replacement historical rate.

For example, a €10 sale on Monday and a partial refund in sats on Tuesday belong to different events. Use their original records when reconciling. Converting both amounts at Friday’s exchange rate would change the meaning of the figures.

Common questions

Why is a restored sale unavailable for refund?

An older backup could omit a refund issued later. Terminal disables refunds from restored sales so a restored record cannot authorize a second payout.

Do I need Pro to give my accountant a CSV?

The raw Sales CSV and date filters are free. Pro adds report comparisons, item performance, presets and report exports.
