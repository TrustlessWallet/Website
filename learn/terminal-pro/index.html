Run a shop with free Terminal

The free Terminal handles keypad checkout, fiat pricing, a five-item catalog, basket orders, the local cashier lock, sales history and date filters. Owners can issue full or partial refunds, share basic text or PDF receipts, export raw Sales CSVs, and create or restore encrypted backups. These are enough to take a first payment and reconcile it afterward.

What Pro adds

A larger catalog. Keep more than five products available for basket checkout.

Reports. Compare periods, explore item performance, save report presets and export report data. Item reports use the product details saved with paid baskets. Keypad-only sales have no product-level breakdown. Item figures are gross, with refunds reported separately.

Receipt design. Add a logo to PDF receipts and use reusable receipt templates.

Purchase or restore access

As the owner, open Terminal Settings → Terminal Pro. Review the local price, billing period and any offer displayed by the App Store. A cashier cannot start a purchase. Use the store purchase restore option with the purchasing account if reinstalling.

Billing uses RevenueCat with an anonymous purchase ID and store transaction information. The billing integration does not send your wallet identifier, seed, balances, sales or receipts. A purchase restores access to features. Local business records travel in your encrypted Terminal backup.

After a subscription expires

You retain access to sales, refunds and raw CSV exports. Existing receipt branding continues to display and can be removed. Saved templates remain stored, and the template library is available with an active subscription. The app does not delete records when subscription status changes.

Common questions

When would a café benefit from Pro?

If staff frequently build baskets from more than five products, or the owner wants to compare product performance across weeks, Pro adds tools for those tasks. A café entering a total on the keypad can already do that for free.

Do I pay a subscription to receive bitcoin?

Basic Terminal checkout is free. Network and payment-service fees can apply to bitcoin operations. Review the terms shown for the operation you are making.
